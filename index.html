About Maison Aurelia

Craft Passed Down Through Generations

Founded in Geneva in 1925, Maison Aurelia brings together master watchmakers and gemologists to create pieces meant to be treasured for life.

Every watch is regulated in-house and every stone is independently graded. We believe true luxury is quiet, precise and built to outlast trends.
